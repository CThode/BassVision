Tritanopia is the absence blue-sensitive (S-cone) cells, causing blues to appear green and yellows/pinks to appear gray or red.
Largemouth and smallmouth both have tritanopic vision
This app uses a tritanopia filter overlayed with a water clarity and depth filter to give you an accurate representation of how a lure would appear to bass.
This app has the ability to save lure photos within the app as well as export the filtered images to your photos and can work without internet.
